Research

ENIAC 2025 · SBC · pp. 676–687

Vision-Language Models for Automated Property Feature Extraction in Tax Assessment: A Case Study

Gustavo R. Ribeiro, Pedro A. M. Saraiva, Luis H. A. Rosa, Enzo L. Marques, Gustavo L. B. Pereira, Pedro M. L. Campos, Luiz M. L. Pascoal, Sávio S. T. de Oliveira

City halls in Brazil send inspectors on site to classify a building's construction features for property tax (IPTU). We built a benchmark grounded in Goiânia's tax code and tested twelve VLMs (Gemini, Gemma, LLaVA, Qwen) zero-shot on listing photos across 11 legal categories, from structure to finishes.
